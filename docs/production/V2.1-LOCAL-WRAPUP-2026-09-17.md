## Research applied

Native looping follows the [AudioBufferSourceNode loop documentation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AudioBufferSourceNode/loopStart). The overlap uses the [equal-power crossfade described by Boris Smus](https://webaudioapi.com/book/Web_Audio_API_Boris_Smus_html/ch03.html) to avoid a level dip where tail and head meet. No additional sound files or libraries were downloaded. Recording provenance and updated playback notes remain in `fresh/dist/data/ambience-sources.json`.
